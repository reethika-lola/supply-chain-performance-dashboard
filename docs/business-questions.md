# Analytical Questions

## Sales

1. What are total revenue, orders, and units sold?
2. How do monthly revenue and order volume change?
3. Which categories, departments, markets, and order regions contribute most revenue?
4. Which products are top performers by revenue and units?
5. What share of sales is contributed by each category?

## Profitability

1. What are total item-level profit and profit margin?
2. Which categories and products have the highest and lowest margins?
3. Are high-revenue products also high-profit products?
4. How does profitability trend month to month?

## Delivery & operations

1. What are average actual and scheduled shipping days?
2. Which markets, regions, categories, and shipping modes have the largest actual-versus-scheduled variance?
3. How does the dataset's `Late_delivery_risk` flag distribute by shipping mode and region?
4. Which delivery statuses occur most often?

> The dataset does not contain an expected delivery date. “On-time” is therefore **not** asserted. The project uses the published late-delivery-risk field and the variance between actual and scheduled shipping days only.

## Geography & customers

1. Which markets and regions generate the most revenue and profit?
2. Are performance differences visible by customer segment?
3. Which destination countries or states have the most order activity?

Inventory and supplier questions are excluded because the selected dataset does not provide actual inventory, supplier, or warehouse entities.
