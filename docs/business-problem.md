# Business Problem

This portfolio analysis uses the DataCo Smart Supply Chain public order-item dataset. It models sales, order activity, item-level profit, delivery timing, product mix, customer segment, and destination geography.

Management needs a compact way to monitor commercial performance and fulfillment operations, then investigate whether specific markets, categories, or shipping modes deserve attention. The project does **not** represent a real-time operational system and does not claim that DataCo Global is a verified operating company.

## Objectives

- Establish a reproducible raw-to-analytics SQL workflow.
- Measure revenue, order volume, units, profit, margin, shipping days, and scheduled-delivery variance.
- Identify trends and top/bottom performers without inventing missing operational data.
- Provide Tableau-ready views and exact workbook build instructions.
