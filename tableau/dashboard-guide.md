# Dashboard Guide

## Supply Chain Executive Overview

KPI cards: total revenue, total orders, total profit, profit margin, average actual shipping days, and late delivery risk %.  
Worksheets: monthly revenue line; revenue by market; revenue by category; delivery status bar; top-products bar.  
Filters: date, market, category, shipping mode.

## Delivery and Operations

Worksheets: shipping-mode comparison; risk by region heatmap; schedule-variance histogram; risk by category; monthly delivery-risk trend.  
Measures: average actual shipping days, average schedule variance days, late-delivery-risk %, and delayed-risk orders.  
Use the source flag faithfully; do not claim on-time delivery.

## Product and Profitability

Worksheets: category revenue/profit; top products by revenue; top products by profit; revenue-versus-profit scatterplot; monthly profit trend.  
Filters: date, market, category.

Use a light background, restrained accent colors, shared filters, concise titles, and filter/highlight actions.
